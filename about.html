Who We Are

A Family Company with Deep Roots in Uganda

Founded in 2015, Pearl Safari Uganda started as a small family-run tour operator with one mission: to show the world the authentic beauty of Uganda — the Pearl of Africa. We believe travel should be seamless, responsible, and genuinely unforgettable.

Today, we are proud to be one of Uganda's most trusted local operators, specialising in gorilla trekking, wildlife safaris, and cultural experiences. Our trips are 100% customisable — we handle every detail from transport to accommodation and permits.

Unlike many online booking platforms, you deal directly with us. No middlemen. No inflated prices. Just honest, local expertise.
