Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

★★★★★

About This Experience

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is one of Africa's most ancient and biologically diverse rainforests, sheltering almost half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas. Located in southwestern Uganda along the rift valley escarpment, Bwindi covers 321 km² of evergreen forest and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Trek through dense jungle with expert rangers to spend a precious, permitted hour face-to-face with a wild gorilla family. It is widely considered one of the most profound wildlife encounters on Earth.

Best Time to Visit

June – August and December – February (dry seasons) offer the best trekking conditions. The forest is accessible year-round, but dry season means firmer trails and clearer views.

3-Day Itinerary

1

Day 1 — Arrival & Orientation

Drive from Kampala or Entebbe to Bwindi (8–9 hrs). Arrive at your forest lodge, settle in, and attend the park briefing at headquarters. Dinner and rest.

2

Day 2 — Gorilla Trek (The Main Event)

Early morning start (7am). Trek 2–6 hours through dense forest with your ranger. Spend one incredible permitted hour with a gorilla family. Afternoon: optional community village walk.

3

Day 3 — Breakfast & Departure

Leisurely breakfast at the lodge. Optional short nature walk before checking out. Drive back to Kampala or onward to your next destination.
